Admin areas — analysis & redesign

Staff & roles · Clients directory · Authentication · Audit logs · Notifications & system config. The pattern this time is different from helpdesk/marketing: most of these pages already read real APIs — but the admin area hides ~1,000 lines of unreachable fiction (Security Center, Integrations, Branches, Feature Flags render mock data and are routed nowhere) and the real pages mix fabricated data into live rows (the audit log injects 4 fake events and stamps every real row with 127.0.0.1). The redesign is primarily about honesty: real columns for real data, dead fiction deleted, and the two colour-leak pages brought back to monochrome.

01 · Staff & roles — /users

The 3,066-line mega-page is mostly real: invite flow, role matrix, per-module toggles all hit /roles and /staff. Three fixes: the directory gets presence and ownership (real staff_presence.lastSeenAt + the conversation-owner role we just built); the "Client accounts" sub-tab is deleted — it embeds the entire ClientDirectory, duplicating /clients under a second nav path; and the invite-success banner stops leaking green.

04 · Audit logs — /audit

The page reads the real settings_audit table — then injects 4 fabricated events (STATIC_SYSTEM_AUDITS: a fake MFA-policy change, a fake role grant, a fake fee update, a fake invitation) and stamps every real row with a fabricated ip: "127.0.0.1". The redesign removes the fiction, derives the category honestly, and extends the writer so actor IP and action are actually recorded instead of guessed from the key name.

07 · Build order

  • 1 · Honesty pass (½ day): delete STATIC_SYSTEM_AUDITS + fabricated IPs, delete the four dead sections + their styles, monochrome the notification pills and auth flash banners, remove the /users "clients" sub-tab.
  • 2 · Audit backend (1 day): settings_audit gains actor_ip + action; admin_audit stream on ban/delete/invite/role-grant/sign-in paths; page gets date + actor filters and pagination.
  • 3 · Staff & auth (1 day): presence + ownership columns, staff record rail, real sessions table + MFA roster on /auth, health-endpoint extension (redis, queue depth, version) feeding a real System overview.
  • 4 · Clients (½ day): kill the 10s poll, branch chips, record pane File/Money/Message using getConversationContext.
  • Total ≈ 3 days, and only two small migrations (actor_ip/action, admin_audit) — everything else renders data that already exists.
