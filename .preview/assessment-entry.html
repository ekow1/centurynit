Assessment form — the entry point reshapes the sections (proposed)

The booking assessment already gates on the entry pick: the four intent cards render alone and the whole form mounts once entryIntent is set. sectionShown / ADMISSIONS_ONLY_SECTIONS hide academics for offer-holders, and ENTRY_DOC_FIELDS swaps the document list. What's missing: the section numbers are literals, a visa-only client is never asked about refusals, a departure client is never asked about arrival, and nothing tells the entrant why their form is shorter. This mock proposes computed numbering, two new sections (visa_history, arrival), and an entry summary strip that makes the shape feel intentional.

A · What's wrong today

Five gaps between intent and form

  • 1 · Number collisions and gaps. Section heads carry literal numbers: 03 · Your offer and 03 · Education both claim 03 (likewise 04 · Your visa / 04 · Employment). For a visa entrant the visible sequence renders 01, 02, 03, 07, 08 — 04–06 vanished but Financial still reads 07, implying hidden work. Numbers must be computed from visibleSections, not literals.
  • 2 · Visa entrants are never asked about visa history. Prior refusals, previous applications, travel history — on a visa-only case that's the single biggest risk input the consultant needs, and the form skips it entirely.
  • 3 · Departure entrants aren't asked what they need on arrival. Housing help, airport pickup, destination city/airport, dependants travelling — only arrivalWindow exists.
  • 4 · No "why we ask less" signal. A visa entrant seeing 6 sections instead of 9 should feel the shape match their purchase — "you only pay for the stretch you ride" → "we only ask for the stretch we work". Today the shorter form reads as missing pages, not a matched scope.
  • 5 · Changing the entry silently hides filled answers. Pick a different intent after typing and whole sections — with their answers — unmount with no warning. Needs a confirm.
