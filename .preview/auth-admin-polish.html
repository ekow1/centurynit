design polish — before / after

Auth brand panel & System Overview

Two fixes. The sign-in page's journey chapter list renders at 0.62rem uppercase mono with 55%-opacity descriptions — under 10px, which is why the middle of the dark panel reads as noise. And the System Overview health cards chop each metric into three thin-bordered bands with inconsistent pills, while the "Configured" card gets squeezed at 2fr so its values clip.
