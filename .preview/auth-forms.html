What changed

1 · The card is gone: no border, no paper background, no shadow around the form. The form sits directly on the stage, so the eye reads the column, not a box.

2 · One sizing system across all nine forms: 46px input height, 46px full width primary, 10px mono uppercase labels, 18px field gaps, one 400px column (560px only for the QR scan row).

3 · Em dashes removed from all copy. Separators are middle dots or sentence breaks.

4 · No back to public site element anywhere. The wordmark stays the quiet way out.

5 · The amber marker stays but moves to the top edge of the primary button, so accent and action always sit together.
