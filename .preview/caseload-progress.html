Proposal

% work done — on the task queue and the caseload

Every number below is computed from data the caseload already holds. A case's % is its chapter position plus the checklist fraction of the current chapter: ((step − 1) + checked/(checked+open)) / total. A consultation's is its status over four steps. An officer's is the mean of their open records. Nothing is estimated; a stalled record's bar simply stops moving.

1 · Officer strip

Load + how far through it

The card keeps open/capacity — that is how much. The new amber line is how far through: mean completion of everything they carry. A full card that is 80% done is a very different problem from a full card at 20%.

The card keeps its chapter label and step count; the new line underneath is the same number drawn. Chapter ticks sit on the bar so you can see where the record is inside its own plan. A stalled record's fill turns red — the bar has literally stopped.

Task groups show done/total

Rules the number keeps
  • Scope-aware. A visa-only case tops out at 3 chapters, so its bar fills faster — the % is against the client's own plan, never the full ladder.
  • Stalled ≠ low %. A case can be 60% done and stalled; the red fill flags the stall, the number stays honest.
  • Completed = 100%. Done records render a full green fill in the completed band.
  • No fake precision. Inside a chapter the fraction comes from real checklist items; when a case has none, the bar sits at the chapter boundary, not midway.
  • Officer % is a mean of open records. It answers "how far through their load" — capacity still answers "how much load."
