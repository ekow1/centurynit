Consultation cancellation & rebooking — proposed

The gaps are all in money and recovery: rebooking always charges the full fee (even when we cancelled on the client), ops can't reopen or issue a free rebook without a debug route, and the cancel confirm doesn't offer the free alternative — reschedule — first. E · The API decision this needs from you
  • Should a rebook ever be free? Proposal: ops can flag a cancellation "our side" (or issue a free-rebooking token) — the client's next checkout then skips Paystack and goes straight to slot + consultation creation. Your call on the rule: e.g. free when ops cancelled, or within N days, or one free rebook per client.
  • Refund path? Nothing refunds today. Options: manual refunds stay ops-side (documented), or Paystack refund API wired to a "Refund & cancel" action.
  • Case linkage: when a client rebooks, link the new consultation to the old (rebooked_from_id) so ops sees one client story, not two orphan rows — the old row's comments/doc requests could carry over too.
  • Small fixes regardless: cancel notification link → /portal/consultation (not the retired /portal/tracking); booking sheet pre-fills branch/type from the cancelled case.
