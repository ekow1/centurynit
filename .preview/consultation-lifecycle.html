B · Confirm click → generate the link

Your correction, confirmed in code: "slot confirmed" is the handler's Confirm slot click in ops (POST /consultations/:id/confirm-slot). And today that endpoint blocks when no link exists — if (online && !booking.meetingUrl) → 409 "Add a meeting link before confirming". Room creation only fires inside assignBooking — so if it failed or wasn't configured at assign time, the handler hits a wall at confirm and has to paste a link by hand.

C · Reschedule → what happens to the link

Answer to "how does reschedule affect the link": by design, it doesn't change — same room, same link, client keeps what they have. What moves is the window, not the address. Per provider:

ProviderOn rescheduleWhy it's correct
LiveKit Nothing to do — room has no baked window The window lives entirely in the JWT minted at each /join — new slot → new token window automatically. The whole nbf-room class of bug can't occur.
Daily PATCH /rooms/{name} re-binds nbf/exp to the new slot (already shipped in e9b24a1) Daily bakes the window into the room at creation — without the patch the room stays locked to the old time.
Manual / Zoom / Meet Link untouched A Zoom link knows nothing about your calendar — it just works whenever opened. Reschedule email still goes out with the same join path.
  • Why it "seemed not to capture": two real causes, both fixed by this plan — (1) unassigned bookings had no room at all (fix B), and (2) pasting a Zoom link left meetingProvider: "livekit" so /join minted a LiveKit token and ignored your link (fix on the companion page — provider flips to "manual" on save).
  • Pending request vs. confirmed move: while a reschedule request is awaiting approval, the old slot — and its room window — stay live. The card shows both times so nobody joins a dead room.
