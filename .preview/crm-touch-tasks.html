Touch log, follow-ups, and why a lead died

Three additions to the lead pane that turn it from a scoreboard into a workstation: Log writes a human touch (call / WhatsApp / email / visit / note) into the lead's trail and moves the real last-client-touch clock; Follow up creates a dated task that lands in the work queue and reminds its owner; Mark lost asks why, so Reports can finally answer the question. new behaviour

1 · The lead pane — before / after

Same rail, three more verbs. The pane stops being read-only: every touch is loggable, the next touch is schedulable, and "lost" costs a reason.

2 · The touch sheet

One small form, four fields, no friction — if logging a call takes longer than the call, staff won't do it.

Log a touchAma Serwaa Boateng
The outcome chips are the scoring data — "no answer ×3" is the honest signal the heat meter can't fake. A follow-up date on the same sheet means the reminder never gets forgotten because logging was easy.

A follow-up is a real row — assignable, dated, reminding through the existing notification pipe (bell + push + email). It files beside the derived tasks, not in a separate screen nobody opens.

4 · Mark lost — the reason is the data

5 · Wiring — small and honest

PieceWhereWhat it is
lead.touchlead_eventsNew event types — touch.call / .whatsapp / .email / .visit / .note with {outcome, body} payload. Same append-only trail, no new table for the log itself.
leads.lastClientTouchAtleads (new column)Written only by touch events and inbound client messages — never by updatedAt. The heat meter and "going cold" band finally measure contact, not edits.
leads.lostReason / lostNoteleads (new columns)Set by the lost dialog; cleared on reopen. Feeds a "why leads die" row in Reports.
tasksnew tabletitle, dueAt, assigneeOpsUserId, leadId?, applicationId?, doneAt, createdBy. The work queue unions derived tasks with real ones; due-soon + overdue rows push through notifyMany → bell, SSE, push.
POST /leads/:id/touchesroutes/leads.tsWrites the event + bumps lastClientTouchAt + optionally creates the follow-up task in one call.
PATCH /leads/:idexistingAlready accepts the fields — the pane just gains an Edit sheet and the lost dialog posts {stage:"lost", lostReason, lostNote}.

Deliberately not in this pass: SMS/WhatsApp sending (separate provider work), public enquiry endpoint, CSV export, merge. Those are their own builds — this one makes the pad honest first.
