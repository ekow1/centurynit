A · The join flow — one button, token under the hood
1 · ClickPortal "Join meeting →" or ops "Join →" — the same button that today opens the raw Meet link.
→
2 · POST /bookings/:id/joinAPI checks who you are: staff on this case, or the client who owns the booking. Anyone else → 403.
→
3 · Mint tokenPOST /meeting-tokens — staff get is_owner; the client gets their name, their user id, and an expiry tied to the slot.
→
4 · window.openhttps://centurynit.daily.co/rm-9k2x?t=eyJ… — Daily's hosted page opens already inside the private room.
  • The URL alone is dead weight. The room is privacy: "private" — no token, no entry, no knocking needed.
  • Tokens are minted per click, never stored. Reschedule the slot and the next mint carries the new window automatically.
  • Backward compatible. Booking's provider is google_meet or a pasted link → /join returns the stored URL untouched.

Join mints your owner token — you land in the room with host controls. The client joins from their portal; the stored room URL works for nobody on its own.
