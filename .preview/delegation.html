Ops · Workspace — proposal

Delegation, rehomed

Delegation was pulled off the case and consultation detail pages — right call: the detail page is where you do the work (call, assess, documents), and delegating is a routing decision a manager makes across cases, not inside one. This proposal gives it three proper homes — the Now rail, the Worklist filter, and the Caseload — plus one shared DelegateSheet. Every scope below already exists in the API; nothing new is needed server-side.

The three scopes the backend already speaks — delegateCoordinator(id, who, note, "case") hands one case over · scope: "journey" / delegateJourney(applicantId, who) hands the applicant's whole journey to a staff member — every case they open inherits them — and releaseJourney takes it back · setDuty(branch, who) routes all of a branch's new cases today. The picker's data comes from getWorkload(): name, role, active cases, overdue, capacity %.

3 · Two levels of manager — the role upgrade

You don't need a new role record — the capability model already splits the two jobs. A branch manager is a manager whose role lacks see_all_branches and whose staff record carries a branch. Everything they can reach — duty, delegation, caseload — is already clamped to that branch by scopeRecords; the only thing missing is enforcing the same clamp on the write routes.

The one server change this needs: the duty, delegate, reassign and release routes currently gate on canSeeAllCases alone — so any manager-level actor can touch any branch. 7 · What the non-manager journey delegation means

QuestionAnswer (all existing behaviour)
Who can a manager delegate to?Anyone coordinator-capable: the coordinator role, or staff with a delegation grant (assertCanCoordinate server-side). Kwame in the mock is a consultant marked delegable — that's your "non-manager staff".
What do they get?The case lands in their queue as coordinatorId, they get the steering notifications, and on the detail page they act as its owner. For a journey delegation, applicants.coordinatorId is set — every future case stamps coordinatedVia = "applicant" automatically.
What can they not do?They can't delegate further or set duty — delegation stays a manager verb. The API already enforces this (Only managers or coordinators… checks on the delegate/duty routes).
Taking it back"Coordinated" chip → select the case → Take back in the sheet (calls reclaimCoordination). Journeys release from the client card via releaseJourney — in-flight cases keep their coordinator, new cases stop routing.
