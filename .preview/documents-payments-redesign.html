Documents & Payments — layout and responsiveness (proposed)

Both pages were built desktop-first around one pattern: content column + rail. On a phone the rail stacks under everything, the Payments table (7 columns, no overflow wrapper) squashes, and the numbers that matter — what needs you, what's due — end up below the fold. This mock re-cuts both pages around one question each: Documents → “what needs me?”; Payments → “what's due now?”

A · What's wrong today

Documents · /portal/documents

  • .dcount is a 4-across band; on mobile it becomes 2×2 but the inverted “Needs you” cell breaks the grid rhythm.
  • Rows are one flat list; chapter grouping exists in data (VaultRow.chapter) but is not expressed visually — verified, in-review, missing and resubmit rows interleave.
  • On desktop the actions column and the inline preview pane compete for width; when the sheet opens the rail disappears — layout jump.
  • Long filenames overflow the mono .drow__file line; upload/replace is a small text button.
  • Sheet: history and note stack below the frame with no tabs — on mobile you scroll past the PDF to reach the note.

Payments · /portal/financial

  • The ledger is a 7-column <table class="ptable"> with no responsive rule — under 900px it just squeezes; .ptable__amt{white-space:nowrap} forces overflow and “Covers” wraps into 3-word columns.
  • Receipts is a second 6-column table with the same problem; date + Paystack reference jammed into one cell.
  • .pposition collapses to three tall stacked blocks — “Due now” sits second as an inverted black bar mid-page.
  • The rail with the Pay → CTA and plan summary is static on mobile — the primary action is at the bottom of a long scroll.
  • Inline-styled <h2>s; “Fee”/“Covers” duplicate the chapter mark; ↓ invoice / ↓ receipt are text-only in the last column and wrap.
  • Payment plan explanation (view="plan") shares the same table.

B — what changed
  • One column of truth: the count becomes a single-line strip and the filter; the rail becomes a persistent preview pane (.dpane, 26rem, sticky) — empty state holds the dropzone + unlock map, so opening a doc never shifts the list.
  • Chapter marks (III/IV/V) head each group; rows order needs-you → in review → verified → missing; reviewer notes are a bordered inset under the row.
  • The sheet gains a Preview · Note · History segmented control and a sticky bottom action bar; Remove demoted to a text link.
  • On mobile: sticky bottom bar carries the next action, chips scroll horizontally, preview and upload are both bottom sheets — thumb stays in the bottom third. Tap targets ≥ 44px; body scroll locked while a sheet is open.

C — what changed
  • .pposition → one inverted Due now block that contains the Pay → CTA, plus a two-cell hairline for Paid / Still to come. When nothing is due the block turns hollow: “Nothing due right now — next fee arrives with Chapter V”.
  • The 7-column table becomes .pledger rows (mark | title | amount | status | actions); Covers + Due + invoice no. fold into a mono sub-line; amounts show USD over GH₵; settled rows dim; the due row keeps the inset-bar treatment.
  • Receipts become a linear list — never a table — collapsed to 3 with “Show all”; the Paystack reference moves to a muted second line.
  • The rail stops repeating the position: one inverted “Next payment” card + “Your plan”, sticky. On mobile the due block leads, Pay is 48px full-width, and a sticky bottom bar appears once you scroll past it.
