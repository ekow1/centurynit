Proposal

The float chat, fixed: AI first, a launcher that actually alerts

Three decisions in one pass. Portal: tabs reorder to AI → Support → Officer (the free instant answer first, humans a tap away), and the launcher earns attention with a real alert state. Requests live inside the float — a list view swaps into the same window; there is no separate portal page. Ops: client support threads leave the staff chat bubble entirely — the standalone desk page owns them; the staff bubble is for colleagues only.

1 · The launcher — five states

Square, and it always says what it's for

Need a hand?Ask the AI — instantor message the desk

Idle — but never dead. A square FAB with a teaser label: it surfaces every ~30s on first visits and on hover after. Even with zero messages, the launcher says what it's for.

Idle, collapsed. After the tease is seen twice it retires to the bare square — no nagging.

Unread. Red count on the FAB — same SSE badge as today, kept.

New reply. Peek card shows who + first line (dismissable), and the FAB pulses amber for ~3s when the message lands. You can't miss it, but it doesn't nag.

Someone's typing. Even closed, the peek shows a live "is typing…" — the desk feels staffed, not abandoned.

3 · Ops — the float is for colleagues, not clients

Client threads leave the staff bubble

Today the ops chat hub mixes staff DMs and client support threads — a consultant's bubble pings for helpdesk work that belongs to the desk queue. Proposed: the float carries staff-only threads (DMs, groups, mentions); every client-facing thread lives on the Helpdesk page where claim/queue state exists. A slim badge still counts the desk — it links to the desk, not into the bubble.
