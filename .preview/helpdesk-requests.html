2b · Raised by the office

Staff can file a request too — for the client, or internally

Two directions, one model. For a client: a phone call or walk-in becomes a tracked request — same queue, same states, visible on the client's portal list marked "logged by the office", and the client is emailed. Internal: agent → IT / finance / manager when work needs an owner and a state — audience: internal keeps it out of the client queue and off the portal entirely. Casual staff asks stay in staff chat; this is for tracked work.
