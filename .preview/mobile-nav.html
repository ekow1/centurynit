Problem · split breakpoints .pbar and the sidebar swap at 959.98px; .tabbar and --shell-bottom swap at 1023.98px. Landscape phones (~930–1024px) and split-screen windows land in the gap: desktop chrome + floating tab bar, no reserved space.
Problem · crowded topbar At these widths the desktop topbar crams breadcrumb + "Next · Pay enrolment →" + bell + "Message Ama" into one row — the row that wraps is the one your screenshot shows straining.
Fix · one breakpoint, one chrome Move the portal's mobile chrome boundary to 1023.98px — same line the tab bar already uses. Below it: sidebar + desktop topbar hide, app bar + tab bar show, --shell-bottom reserves real space. Above it: pure desktop, no tab bar. No dead zone.
Fix · short-height trim On landscape-height screens (max-height: 520px) the "Next" strip folds into the app bar as a compact chip, and the kicker drops — the bar stops eating 15% of a 500px screen.
