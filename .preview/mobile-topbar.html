Portal mobile app bar — one line, hollow marks, quieter badge (proposed)

The phone app bar (.pbar) packs a solid CN block, a two-line title, a bell wearing its unread count on top of the glyph, and a second identical black square for the account avatar — all inside 56px. This mock keeps the row and the ≥44px tap targets but gives each element its own weight: hollow brand mark, single-line title with an inline chapter chip, a corner badge that clears the bell, and an outlined avatar that reads as a button.

A · What's squished today

One 56px sticky row, five occupants

  • 1 · Density. A 34px solid-black CN square, the title Journey (1.05rem) with VI · COMPLETE (0.6rem uppercase mono) stacked beneath it, a 44px bell button with an absolutely-positioned 22 badge overlapping the icon, and a 26px EE initials square inside a 44px button — all inside one 56px row on a ~360px-wide screen.
  • 2 · Two identical black squares. The CN brand mark and the EE avatar carry the same weight — brand and identity read as the same affordance, and the avatar doesn't look tappable.
  • 3 · Title-height-driven row. The kicker stacks under the title, so the row is sized by two text lines; with the Next strip (pbar__next) the bar can eat ~110px+ at the top of every page.
  • 4 · Badge leak + overlap. The bell count uses a hardcoded 18181b inline style (not a token) at top:2px; right:2px — it overlaps the bell glyph and reads as clutter rather than "22 unread". (Sibling leak: .fab__badge still uses e53935 — both are the current colour leaks this proposal removes.)
  • 5 · Stage pages. The leading square becomes a back chevron (.pbar__back, 44px) — good — but the row still carries title + kicker + bell + avatar.

What changed

  • Single-line title. Journey (1.05rem) sits on one line with a small uppercase mono chip VI · COMPLETE inline (1px border, 2px 6px padding). The stacked second line is gone; long kickers truncate inside the chip.
  • Hollow brand mark. CN shrinks to 30px and swaps the solid fill for a 1.5px border — it stops colliding with the avatar.
  • Outlined avatar. EE becomes a 28px outlined square (1.5px border, transparent bg, foreground initials) inside the same 44px button — reads as a control, distinct from the brand.
  • Badge anchored to the corner. The count is a 14px-min square in solid foreground at the button's top-right (0.55rem mono), ringed with background so it clears the bell glyph — and it uses var(--foreground)/var(--background) tokens, not 18181b.
  • Breathing room. The row keeps ~56px but gains padding-block:6px, so glyphs don't touch the bar's edges.
