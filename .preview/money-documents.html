journey portal · money documents — before / after

Invoice and receipt, together

Today a paid invoice makes the invoice document vanish and only the receipt remains; unpaid rows show a Pay button with no way to see the bill itself. And both "downloads" are actually a print-dialog window, not the real PDF the API already emails. This pass puts Invoice · Receipt side by side on every paid row, adds the invoice link everywhere an invoice exists, itemizes every payment on the receipt, and serves the real server-generated PDF.

4 · The rest of the sweep

Smaller gaps the same pass closes

  • Fees chapter rail. PortalFeesChapter shows CN-… as dead text — the invoice number becomes a link pair: ↓ invoice · ↓ receipt.
  • Complete chapter. "Money, in full" rows get ↓ invoice ↓ receipt next to each total, replacing the lone "receipt" jlink.
  • Dead link. The awaiting-handler "Deposit receipt" button points at /portal/money — no such route, it silently redirects home. Repoint to /portal/financial or drop it for a direct receipt link.
  • Proforma stays clean. Raised-but-unapproved invoices show no document links — there's no issued bill to print yet. The hint text already explains.
  • Voided invoices vanish. Already filtered out everywhere; the PDF endpoint 404s them too.

Server side the work is one route — GET /api/v1/me/invoices/:id/pdf?kind=invoice|receipt — streaming the buffers generateInvoicePdf / generateReceiptPdf already produce for settlement emails. Everything else is putting existing buttons where the invoices already render.
