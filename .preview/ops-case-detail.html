WHAT CHANGES. (1) The tab row becomes the chapter spine. Today the tabs are eight equal words with a small dot for "now" and a lock glyph — the spine gives them the portal's roman numerals: done chapters carry ✓, the current one is inverted (and is the page's "you are here"), locked ones dim with their unlock reason on hover. Money and Documents aren't chapters — they drop to the right as utility cells (₵ / ▤), not fake stages. (2) The header keeps CaseHeader's fields but drops the nested card-in-card: one bordered head — who, the mono fact line, and the people column (consultant + change, other officers, History/Assign). (3) NextActionBand is already right — blocked = inverted row, waiting = pill. It stays. (4) Locked chapters no longer dead-end into "Overview": hovering a locked cell shows why ("Unlocks on the first admission"), clicking a locked cell does nothing instead of navigating away.

WHAT IT FIXES. A handler opening a case currently can't tell chapter progress without reading each tab; the spine shows the whole journey at a glance — the same I–VI vocabulary the client's portal uses, so "the client is in Chapter III" means the same thing on both sides.
