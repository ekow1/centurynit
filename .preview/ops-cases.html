WHAT CHANGES. (1) The inline-styled scope banner — inverted for managers, grey for officers — becomes one count band: in-view count, current chapter, work-waiting count, the inverted needs-an-owner cell, and the "My cases" toggle as a band cell instead of a floating button. (4) Board view unchanged — same filters now, same columns.
