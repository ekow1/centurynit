WHAT CHANGES. (1) Color out — the green/red metric cards become one count band (the suspended cell inverts instead of going red); ACTIVE green pill → solid monochrome; the banned row's red wash → the inset ink bar + underlined pill; colored buttons (amber Revoke, dark-red Delete, green Unban) → underlined text links. (2) The table becomes a ledger — hairline rows, mono dates, stage as a hollow pill that says the chapter ("Client · Ch III") instead of bare "Lead". (3) A record pane — today there is nowhere to look at one client: ban/revoke/delete fire from a row's buttons. Selecting a row opens the record: access facts, the case link, sessions with revoke, and the danger zone at the bottom with the same three delete choices. (4) "CRM Stage" column merges into the stage pill — lead stage vs applicant status stop competing.
