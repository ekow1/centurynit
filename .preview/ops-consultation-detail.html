E3 · Delegate — the intended model is a handover, not a label

The intent (per your description): the manager hands case coordination to a coordinator, who then does the assigning and drives the journey stage → finish. E4 · how delegation gets applied — three scopes that nest

Per your scenarios: delegate one case, delegate a person's whole journey, or put someone on duty for the day's intake. They're not alternatives — they nest, and the case resolves them in a fixed order.

ScopeRuleWhere it lives
Case — "Kwame runs this case" Explicit handover of one case. Overrides anything below it. consultations.coordinatorId — exists today
Applicant — "Kwame runs Enoch's journey" Every case this person ever opens — consultation, a free rebook's new case, the application — inherits him. This is what "stage to finish" actually requires. applicants.coordinatorId — new field; cases inherit it at creation
Duty — "Kwame runs today's intake" Coverage, not supervision: cases created at a branch on a date route to the duty coordinator automatically. End of duty routes only new cases — in-flight cases stay with whoever holds them. coordinator_duty — new table: branch · date · coordinator · setBy

Resolution order — stamped on the case at creation

1  case delegated by hand  → wins, always
2  applicant has a coordinator  → journey stays coordinated across rebooks & the application
3  branch has a duty coordinator today  → coverage for walk-ins & new bookings
4  none  → the case is uncoordinated; managers steer as today

The case stores who coordinates it and how they got it (coordinatedVia: case | applicant | duty) — so the detail page can say "Coordinated by Kwame · via today's duty" and a reclaim knows what it falls back to.

S4 · standing grant — authority, not routing

The fourth scenario is a different layer: "grant a staff member access to manage cases until I retract it." S1–S3 decide which cases a person coordinates; S4 decides who may coordinate at all. It isn't a new mechanism — ops_users.permissions + dynamic roles already express it. What it lacks is a proper control surface and an audit trail.
