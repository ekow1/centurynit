Portal contrast & hierarchy — proposed fixes

Home (DashboardHome) + Appointments (PortalAppointments), rendered as the Enoch screenshots show them. Root cause of the wash-out: nearly every label is --muted-foreground (#52525b) at 0.56–0.68rem in mono caps — a mid-gray that's fine at body size but dissolves at micro sizes. The fix isn't one global darker gray; it's a tiered micro-label style (darker ink + slightly larger size) plus real presence for the live surfaces.

1 · Chapter strip — locked chapters shouldn't be ghosts

"Departure" and "Complete" are dashed-border + #a3a3a3-adjacent ink: they read as disabled to the point of invisibility. Locked should mean quiet but legible — a thin solid border, readable label.

7 · Structure — dead space & rail weight

Both pages end the left column early while the rail runs long. Two cheap fixes, no re-architecture: the "From your file" updates card moves into the rail under Documents on Home (it's reference material, not the page's job), and on Appointments the "How rescheduling works" + "Cancelling" cards merge into one "Good to know" card. Left column then owns the live content; the rail owns reference.

Also: "consultation" service names render sentence-cased ("Consultation") in the hero and table — lowercase read as a styling accident next to every other bold label.

Proposed order

  1. Micro-label tier: .pkv__k, .ptable th, .jmini__st, .ptable__sub, .pnext__meta → 0.66–0.68rem, #3f3f46
  2. Locked states: jmini + sidebar — solid thin borders, per-element alpha instead of whole-row opacity
  3. Money rail card: MoneyInline → stacked GHS-primary treatment
  4. Now section: lead-line weight + foot separation
  5. Appointment hero: fact row + ghost buttons + inverted date tile
  6. Rail consolidation (moves, no restyle)

Nothing here changes the token system itself — --muted-foreground stays for body-size secondary text. The new tier only applies to sub-0.7rem labels, which is where #52525b fails. All monochrome; no new colors introduced.
