Methodology

How the process works

Four disciplined stages, from first consultation to departure. Every stage has named deliverables and a dedicated advisor.

  1. 1–2 sessions→

    01

    Counsel

    One-on-one at our Accra or Kumasi office, or online. Academic background, budget and ambitions mapped to the right programme.

    • Study-abroad roadmap
    • Destination shortlist
    • Budget & funding plan
  2. 2–6 weeks→

    02

    Admit

    Applications prepared to each university's exact requirements — transcripts, statements, references — tracked to an offer.

    • Application file per uni
    • SOP coaching
    • Offer tracking
  3. 2–8 weeks→

    03

    Visa

    Embassy-ready file assembled — financial evidence, CAS/LOA, forms filed. Mock interviews before the real one.

    • Embassy-ready file

    04

/faqsanswers

Questions

Straight answers.

How much does the consultation cost?+

One consultation — online or in person at Accra or Kumasi. The fee is shown before you pay; it is the only charge until you decide to enrol.

Do you guarantee a visa?+

No consultancy can. What we guarantee is a complete, consistent, credible file — and that you see every step of it in your portal.

How do I follow my application?+

Every client gets a live portal: stage tracking, invoices, documents and chat with your officer. No WhatsApp chases.

Which countries do you cover?+

UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Germany and the UAE — over 100 partner institutions.

FAQs become real accordions with the site's ruled rhythm — answers that mention the portal and the consultation fee double as conversion copy.
