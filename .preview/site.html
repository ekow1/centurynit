Public website — services-forward redesign

The site's job is to convert a visitor into a booked consultation. Today the services — the actual product — are five generic cards at section 3 of 9, their substance (deliverables, duration, process) hidden behind clicks. Meanwhile the strongest proof — the client portal itself — is invisible.

Findings

Services undersoldFive cards, one sentence each, Learn more →. The deliverables ("embassy-ready visa file", "mock interview", "shortlist in 5 working days") — the things that actually convert — only exist on detail pages.
Homepage is a brochure wall9 sections: hero → stats → spotlight → services → destinations → universities → programs → red seat → methodology → scholarships → CTA → articles. Four grids of near-identical media cards blur together.
The portal never appears"One journey — consultation through visa — in your dashboard" is the CTA band's claim, but nothing shows it. Every competitor says "we keep you updated"; only you can show live case tracking.
No price anchorThe consultation fee exists in the fee catalogue but nowhere on the site. "How much does it cost?" is the first question; silence reads as expensive or evasive.
Red Seat films are placeholdersvideoUrl: "" on all six — the code comment says "swap before this goes live." They're live. Play buttons open an empty lightbox.
Weak stats"Founded 2011 · 2 branches · 6+ destinations · 100+ partners" — input numbers. No outcome proof: visas granted, success rate, students placed.
KeepThe hero carousel (strong), the square/rule design language, the methodology spine (4 stages), the written testimonials.
