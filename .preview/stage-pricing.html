Choose the stages, pay for the stages

Today a package is a funding track with one flat price for the whole journey and a 10 / 30 / 60 payment split gated on visa approval and arrival. This mock separates track (how hard Century searches) from scope (which stages Century does), prices each stage, and lets the milestones follow the scope. Numbers are illustrative.

The gap

The split is an affordability plan for a client who goes all the way, not a price for the work. Admissions is the heaviest stage and is paid at 10 %. A visa refusal after full admissions work leaves Century with $150 and an "issued" invoice that never resolves — there is no rule in the code for a case that stops.
