Visa tracking & Applications — layout and shared timeline (proposed)

The visa page states the same facts four times and still reads "in progress" after the grant. The applications page repeats its CTA and keeps offering a switch after visa processing began. And the shared timeline leaks colour, repeats edited briefings verbatim, and files Chapter V updates under Chapter III. This mock gives each page one state block, one readable step strip, and a timeline that coalesces.

A · What's wrong today

Visa · /portal/visa

  • The same facts render three times: the black "Current update" banner, the 4-step spine (dates in each step), and the rail's "The case file" pkv — then the timeline repeats them a fourth time.
  • Complete still looks in-progress: step 4 "Visa granted · now" narrates the process though validFrom/validTo/collectedAt exist. No settled hero.
  • "Your visa officer" prints a raw email as the name; the role line is generic.
  • "Your visa documents · 1/5 verified" is a stale checklist competing with the outcome; per-row action is a tiny mono "UPLOAD" text link; the CTA goes to the vault without preselecting the doc.
  • "After the visa" is a paragraph, not a next action. The 4-step spine spends a lot of vertical space on four lines.

Applications · /portal/application

  • "Visa processing requested" appears twice with the same CTA (journey-now banner + section 3 card).
  • The .psteps4 labels are ~0.6rem mono — unreadable; the useful facts under them are tiny.
  • Two equal-weight school cards though one is accepted; the loser still offers "Switch to this offer" after visa processing began; a mini 3-column offer table per card; "arrives once <email> uploads it" printed twice.
  • Section 2 "Application invoice" is a single sentence — no invoice number, no receipt link.
  • Rail "This chapter" uses the handler's email as a value; "How accepting works" is irrelevant once accepted.

Shared · ConsultantUpdates

  • Colour leaks: fresh-item red gradient, #92400e doc-req amber, #0d7a3f decision green, #5b21b6 assign purple.
  • Repeated entries: five near-identical departure-briefing blocks (every staff edit re-emits the whole briefing); two identical "Visa appointment" lines.
  • Wrong chapter: Chapter V briefing lines render under Chapter III "Updates" — the filter is just !isVisaUpdate(c).
  • Author shows "Super Admin" or a raw email. Multi-line bodies are a raw text block.

D — what changed
  • All markers monochrome shapes (solid / hollow / diagonal / dotted / dashed squares); kind chips are outline only; "new since last visit" is a hairline rule, no gradient.
  • coalesce(): same author + same kind + ≥80% identical text within 24h → one entry with updated ×5 · last 19 Sep 10:12 and a "show edits" toggle.
  • Multi-line bodies render as lists, max 4 lines then "+N more".
  • Chapter routing by content: departure-briefing lines (Report to the school / Orientation / Airport pickup / Accommodation / Emergency contact / Arriv*) file under Chapter V only; visa lines under IV; the rest under III.
  • Author is always a display name; super-admin edits read "Century NIT".
